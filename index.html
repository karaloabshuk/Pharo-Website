About Us

Pharo Fantasy is your fantasy football headquarters. Build a dream squad of real footballers, manage a $50M budget, pick your starting XI and bench, choose a captain, and climb the league leaderboard every matchday.

⚽

Build Your Squad

Pick your starting XI and bench from real players, each with a market price. Every pick costs money, so balance stars with clever bargains.

🥇

Pick a Captain

Tap any player on the pitch to hand them the armband. Your captain earns double points and drives your matchday score.

🔁

Make Substitutions

Swap underperforming starters for your bench at any time. When a captain is subbed off, the player replacing them takes the armband.

📈

Use Transfers

Search the transfer market, add and remove players, and keep your team value healthy while staying inside the salary cap.
